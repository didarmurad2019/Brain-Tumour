# Brain Tumour -- Supporting Information

This repository contains the supplementary information associated with
the research manuscript:

**"Temporal decoupling of CTLA-4 and ERK sensitivity in brain tumour
signaling: a theoretical basis for sequencing checkpoint and MAPK
inhibition."**

The supplementary materials provide additional mathematical
formulations, model parameters, initial conditions, immunotherapy
specifications, and machine-learning performance assessments supporting
the theoretical and computational analyses presented in the manuscript.

### Supplementary File

**Supplementary File S1: ODE Equations**

This document presents the system of ordinary differential equations
(ODEs) governing the CTLA-4-mediated signaling pathway, including the
mathematical formulations underlying the computational model.

## Research Scope

The supplementary information supports the investigation of temporal
interactions between CTLA-4-mediated immune checkpoint regulation and
ERK-associated MAPK signaling in brain tumour dynamics. It provides
additional quantitative and mathematical details relevant to the model
structure, parameterization, computational evaluation, and theoretical
investigation of the sequencing of checkpoint inhibition and
MAPK-targeted interventions.
